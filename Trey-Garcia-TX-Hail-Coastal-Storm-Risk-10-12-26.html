03

Scoring system

Public federal data at census-tract resolution

What goes into the score
  • HailNRI hail risk: how often damaging hail falls times the value exposed, scored 0 to 100 nationally. Half of Texas housing scores above 88.
  • TornadoNRI tornado risk: shown in the tables and the spreadsheet.
  • CoastalNRI coastal flood and hurricane risk: used to flag the coastal ZIPs in section 05.
  • TiersBecause almost all of Texas is above the national median for hail, the tiers are set within Texas: Extreme hail score 97+, High 93 to 97, Moderate 80 to 93, Low under 80.
Reference points
  • Houston and coastHouston 77002: hail 30 (Low); Galveston 77550: hail 41 (Low); Corpus Christi 78401: hail 71 (Low)

05

Houston and the coast

Your home market · Harris · Galveston · Brazoria · Fort Bend · Montgomery

Coastal ZIPInland ZIP
Coastal ZIPs have a coastal flood score of 50+ or at least 20% of residents in tracts rated high for coastal flooding. Friendswood, Clear Lake, League City and the Galveston Bay shore are coastal by this measure; Katy, Spring, Tomball and Conroe are inland.
What the numbers say
  • Your leads256 of 686 leads (37%) came from Houston Metro, which holds 24% of Texas households; 103 of them are Home leads (47% of Home).
  • Hail is lowerEvery Houston Metro ZIP but one is Low for hail. The exposure here is hurricane wind and coastal flooding, which land on the Home policy.
  • Your officeFriendswood 77546 scores 91 for hurricane and 81 for coastal flooding, with a median household income of $123,756.
  • Flood with Home55 Home leads came from coastal ZIPs. Standard homeowners policies exclude flood; quote it on those leads in the first call.

07

Hail in the conversation

Roofs on Home, comprehensive on Auto

Why it matters here
  • CoverageHail damage to a car is paid under comprehensive coverage. A shopper switching on liability only has no hail protection; ask about it on every quote.
  • DeductiblesIn High and Extreme ZIPs the comprehensive deductible is worth explaining at quote time, before the first spring storm.
  • RoofsRoof age and material drive Home price and wind and hail deductibles in Texas; ask on every Home quote.
